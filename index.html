About

A compact cassette deck for your own audio files. Import tracks from your device or drop them onto the player. The reels turn and the tape packs wind across as the track plays, and the VU meter reads the real left and right levels of the signal.

The tape that ships in the deck is a short demo synthesized in your browser, so you can try every control before importing music.

Controls

  • Album art opens the track editor. The ⋯ button opens the menu.
  • Bottom bar: ◀ rewinds 10 s, ● stops and returns to the start, ■ opens the library.
  • Previous restarts the track when more than 3 s have played, otherwise goes back one track.

Limits

  • Embedded tags and cover art are read from MP3 files (ID3v2). Other formats use the file name as "Artist - Title"; fix details or add a cover in the track editor.
  • Your library is stored in this browser only (IndexedDB). Clearing site data removes it, and it does not sync to other devices.
  • Fullscreen and lock-screen media controls depend on the browser and are not available everywhere.
  • Formats play only if your browser supports them; unsupported files are reported and skipped.

    Install on iPad

    Installed. You're running AEM Cassette Player from your Home Screen.

    This copy runs inside Claude's preview, so Safari would save the Claude page, not the player. Open your own hosted copy of the player in Safari, then follow the steps below.

    iPad and iPhone (Safari)

    1. Open the player's address in Safari.
    2. Tap Share in the toolbar.
    3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then tap Add.
    4. Start the player from the new icon. It opens full screen, without browser bars, in portrait or landscape.

    Good to know

    • The installed app keeps its own library. Import your music again after installing.
    • After the first launch it opens without a network connection.
    • Keep the app open for continuous playback; iPadOS may pause audio when the app is in the background.
